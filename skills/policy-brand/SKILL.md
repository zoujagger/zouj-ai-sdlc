# Brand & voice (advisory)

<!-- Replace with your brand guidelines. -->

- Voice: clear, friendly, confident. No jargon in user-facing text.
- Product names are always written as: <ProductName>.
- Sentence case for headings and buttons.
- Inclusive language: avoid idioms, gendered defaults, and ableist terms.
- Release notes lead with user benefit, not implementation detail.
